# Math Source Audit

## Independence

Use a fresh context when practical. Read the candidate statement and sources, not the scout's persuasive reasoning. The auditor may reject, narrow, split, or mark status unresolved.

## Required checks

1. Resolve the original statement and definitions from primary sources.
2. Record an explicit `as_of` date and search scope.
3. Locate later results, solved variants, equivalent formulations, counterexamples, and terminology drift.
4. Distinguish exact problem, stronger/weaker variants, special cases, and project-internal derived frontiers.
5. Verify that domain, quantifier order, assumptions, evidence standard, and completion standard can be frozen without inventing missing content.
6. Return `PASS`, `FAIL`, or `INCONCLUSIVE` with exact source locators, checked scope, unresolved items, and reopen/refresh conditions.

## Authority

A PASS authorizes objective-freeze preparation only. It does not prove mathematical openness forever, create a Project, choose a route, or grant completion authority. Save the receipt inside the future Project or its pre-genesis source area and bind it from the registry entry.
