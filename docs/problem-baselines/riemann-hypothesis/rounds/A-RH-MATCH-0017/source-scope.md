## Non-implications

The exact finite solver does not accept actual irrational zeros as certified
inputs without an additional interval/enclosure bridge. No zero enumeration was
performed. A minimum of a conservative linear deletion surrogate is not a minimum
of the true quadratic charge or a signed operator norm. Large upper charges do
not become lower operator-energy bounds. Rational flow certificates concern finite
supplied graphs; they do not certify a universal source matching theorem, an
independent mathematical receipt, an unconditional zero proportion or RH.
